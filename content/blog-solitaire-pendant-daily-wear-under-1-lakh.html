Design Variations

Classic 4-Prong Solitaire

Four prongs holding a single diamond — the purest expression of the solitaire concept. The prongs allow maximum light to enter the stone from all angles, creating the most brilliant sparkle. This is our most popular pendant design at Auric Jewels.

Bezel-Set Solitaire

The diamond is enclosed in a thin metal rim. This creates a sleek, modern look and protects the diamond's edges from impact. Ideal for active women who want zero-maintenance daily wear. The bezel also makes the diamond appear slightly larger.

Halo Pendant

A centre diamond surrounded by a ring of smaller diamonds. A 0.20 ct centre stone with a halo looks equivalent to a 0.40 ct solitaire — making this the value-maximising design. Budget: ₹40,000-₹80,000 for a pendant that looks twice its carat weight.

Drop Pendant

The diamond hangs freely from the chain via a small bail, creating gentle movement and light play as you move. Pear and marquise shapes work especially well as drops, elongating the neckline and creating a sophisticated silhouette.

How to Choose the Right Specifications

Cut — Prioritise Above All

In a solitaire pendant, cut quality is everything. The pendant catches light from above (room lighting) rather than from the side (like rings). An Excellent cut diamond maximises the light return from above, creating visible sparkle across a room. Never compromise on cut to buy a larger stone.

Colour — G or H

At pendant distance (12-18 inches from the viewer's eye), colour differences are virtually invisible. G and H colour diamonds appear white and bright. Going below I colour may show warmth, particularly in white gold settings.

Clarity — SI1 Is Fine

A pendant diamond is viewed from a greater distance than a ring diamond. Inclusions that might be visible in a ring at close inspection are completely invisible in a pendant. SI1 clarity is more than sufficient for daily wear pendants, saving you 15-20% compared to VS2.

Chain — The Unsung Hero

The chain matters. At Auric Jewels, every solitaire pendant comes with a matched 18K gold chain — selected for appropriate thickness (thin enough to be elegant, strong enough for daily wear), length (16-18 inches for optimal pendant positioning), and clasp type (lobster clasp for security).
