What to Look for in a Gurgaon Jewellery Showroom

BIS Hallmarking on Every Piece

Since 2021, every piece of gold jewellery sold in India must carry a BIS hallmark with a HUID number. Verify this at any showroom you visit. At Auric Jewels, every gold piece — from a 2-gram nose pin to a 100-gram bridal set — carries full BIS certification.

Diamond Certification (Not In-House Grading)

Insist on IGI or GIA certification for every diamond. Avoid showrooms that offer only "in-house quality guarantee" — this is not certification. At Auric Jewels, every diamond above 0.20 carats is independently certified by IGI or GIA.

Transparent Pricing

Your bill should itemise gold weight, gold rate, diamond weight, diamond rate, making charges, wastage, and GST as separate line items. If a showroom gives you a single number without breakdown — that is a red flag.

After-Sale Service

Choose a showroom that offers polishing, resizing, restringing, and exchange services. Jewellery is a long-term relationship, not a one-time transaction. Auric Jewels offers free lifetime polishing, resizing, and exchange at full gold value on all purchases.
