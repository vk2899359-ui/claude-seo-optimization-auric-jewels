Top Gold Chain Trends for Women in 2026

1. Chunky Link Chains

Bold, oversized link chains in 22K and 18K gold are the breakout trend of 2026. The layered look — two to four chains of different lengths worn together — remains one of the strongest trends. Pre-styled layered sets from Auric Jewels take the guesswork out: chains are designed to sit at complementary lengths (14", 16", 18", 22") without tangling. Available in 18K gold. 3. Paperclip Chains

Elongated, flat links resembling paperclips create a modern, architectural aesthetic. These chains offer substantial visual presence with surprisingly low weight because the links are hollow or flat. 4. Diamond Station Chains

A delicate gold chain punctuated by evenly spaced diamond stations — each station featuring a single bezel-set diamond. This design blends the warmth of gold with the sparkle of certified diamonds. The most wearable diamond jewellery format for daily wear. Weight: 5-10 grams. Price: ₹75,000-₹2,00,000 depending on diamond count and size.

5. Figaro and Cuban Link

Figaro chains (alternating long and short links) and Cuban link chains (interlocking rounded links) are having a revival, especially in heavier weights. Originally popular in men's jewellery, women in 2026 are wearing them in 18K yellow gold as power accessories. Weight: 12-25 grams. Price: ₹1,40,000-₹3,60,000.

6. Herringbone Chains

Flat, V-shaped links creating a smooth, fluid chain that lays perfectly flat against the skin. Herringbone chains in 18K gold are strikingly elegant and catch light beautifully due to their flat surface. Weight: 8-15 grams. Price: ₹95,000-₹2,15,000.

22K vs 18K — Which Gold for Chains?

For standalone gold chains (no diamonds), 22K gold delivers the richest colour and highest gold value. For chains with diamond stations or delicate designs, 18K gold offers better durability and the option of white or rose gold. At Auric Jewels, we stock both purities and advise based on the specific design and your intended use.

How to Style Gold Chains in 2026

Office Look

A single delicate 18K chain (16 inches) with a small diamond station or pendant. Professional, understated, and elevated.

Weekend Casual

A chunky link chain or paperclip chain over a plain t-shirt or linen shirt. The chain IS the outfit's statement piece.

Indian Ethnic

A layered set of 22K gold chains in varying lengths with a silk saree or kurta. Traditional warmth meets contemporary styling.

Evening / Party

A bold Cuban link or herringbone chain paired with diamond earrings. Let the chain do the talking at the neck; let diamonds sparkle at the ears.
