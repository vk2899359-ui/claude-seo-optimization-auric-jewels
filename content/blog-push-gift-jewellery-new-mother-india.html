Push Gift Jewellery for New Mothers — Diamond & Gold Ideas 2026

The push gift — a piece of jewellery given to a new mother to celebrate the birth of her child — has become one of India's fastest-growing jewellery gifting traditions. What started as a Western practice has been embraced by Indian families, particularly in metropolitan cities like Gurgaon and Delhi NCR, as a meaningful way to honour a mother's journey. This guide from Auric Jewels covers the best push gift jewellery ideas, budgets, and what makes each piece special.

What Is a Push Gift?

A push gift (also called a push present or baby gift for mom) is a piece of jewellery given to a new mother — typically by her partner — to mark the birth of a baby. It celebrates her strength, her sacrifice, and the beginning of a new chapter. In India, this tradition beautifully complements existing customs of gifting gold during a baby's birth.

When to Buy a Push Gift

The best time to buy is before the baby arrives — during the third trimester. This gives you time to choose carefully, customise if needed, and have the gift ready for the birth day. If the baby has already arrived, it is never too late. Many fathers buy the push gift within the first few weeks and present it during a quiet, private moment at home.
