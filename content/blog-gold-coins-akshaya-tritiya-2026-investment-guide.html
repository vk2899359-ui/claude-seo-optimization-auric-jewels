How to Verify Gold Coin Authenticity

BIS Hallmark

Every gold coin sold in India should carry a BIS hallmark confirming purity. For 24K coins, look for "999" or "999.9" fineness marking. The hallmark includes a BIS logo, purity number, and assaying centre mark. At Auric Jewels, every coin carries full BIS certification.

Tamper-Proof Packaging

Genuine gold coins are sealed in assay-certified blister packaging. The packaging should not be opened, bent, or resealed. If a seller offers loose coins without packaging, treat it as a warning sign.

Weight Verification

Always insist on weighing the coin on a certified electronic scale at the point of purchase. The weight should match the denomination exactly. Auric Jewels weighs every coin in front of the customer before billing.
