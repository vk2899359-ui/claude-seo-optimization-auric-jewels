### Slide 2 — What Is BIS Hallmark
**Text:** "What the BIS Hallmark Means"
- BIS Logo — Bureau of Indian Standards certified
- Purity grade — 22K (916), 18K (750), 14K (585)
- HUID — 6-digit unique number for every piece
- Assaying centre mark
**Visual:** Annotated diagram of a hallmark stamp with labels.

### Slide 3 — How to Verify
**Text:** "How to Check Your HUID Number"
1. Find the HUID on your jewellery
2. Visit bis.gov.in/verify-huid
3. Enter the 6-digit alphanumeric code
4. View jeweller details, purity, and weight
**Visual:** Phone screen showing BIS verification page.

### Slide 4 — Red Flags
**Text:** "Warning Signs of Unhallmarked Gold"
- No HUID number visible
- Seller refuses to provide hallmark certificate
- Price significantly below market rate
- "KDM" marking (outdated, not BIS compliant)
**Visual:** Red warning icons next to each point.

## 5. FACEBOOK POST 2 (Engagement Style)

Have you ever verified the hallmark on your gold jewellery?

Here is how: look for the HUID number (6 digits, usually on the inside of the piece), visit bis.gov.in, and enter the code. You will see the exact gold purity, weight, and the jeweller who sold it.

If you cannot find a HUID number, that is a red flag worth investigating.
