1. Are the Making Charges Disclosed Upfront?

Making charges are the single biggest source of buyer complaints in the Gurgaon jewellery market. Customers walk in seeing a gold price, select a piece, and only at billing discover that making charges of 15% to 22% — plus a separate polishing charge, plus GST — have been added on top.

This is not illegal. But it is deeply unfair — and entirely avoidable.

Before you look at a single piece, ask: "What are your making charges per gram, and are they fixed or variable by design?"

A trustworthy showroom will answer this immediately and clearly. If the answer is vague, or if you are told you will see it on the bill, that is your signal to proceed with caution.

At Auric Jewels, Sector 45, making charges are disclosed before billing — always. You know exactly what you are paying before you say yes.

2. Is the BIS Hallmark Genuine — and Can You Verify It?

The Bureau of Indian Standards hallmark is mandatory for gold jewellery above a certain weight sold in India. Every hallmarked piece carries a six-digit HUID — Hallmark Unique Identification number — that you can verify yourself using the BIS Care app on your phone.

Weight fraud is a documented issue in the Gurgaon jewellery market. A piece described as one weight at the point of selection can sometimes be billed at a different weight. BIS hallmarking with HUID verification is the only way to independently confirm what you are buying.

Before purchase, ask: "Can I see the BIS hallmark and HUID number on this piece?" Then verify it yourself on the BIS Care app before you pay.

Every gold piece at Auric Jewels — whether 18K or 22K — carries a verifiable BIS hallmark with HUID. We encourage customers to check it themselves.

3. Is There an IGI or GIA Certificate for Every Diamond?

Diamonds are priced on four variables: cut, colour, clarity, and carat weight. Without a grading certificate from a recognised laboratory, you are trusting a salesperson's description of something worth tens of thousands of rupees.

The International Gemological Institute (IGI) and the Gemological Institute of America (GIA) are the two most trusted diamond grading labs globally. Their certificates are independently issued, cannot be faked, and travel with the diamond permanently.

Ask before purchase: "Is there an IGI or GIA certificate for this diamond, and does it come with my purchase?"

If the answer is no, or if you are offered a certificate from an unknown lab, treat it as a warning sign. At Auric Jewels, every diamond comes with an original IGI or GIA certificate. It is yours to keep.

Gold jewellery is not a one-time purchase. Rings need resizing. Pieces get worn and need checking. Life circumstances change and jewellery is exchanged or upgraded.

5. Can You Get the Gold Rate Confirmed Before You Visit?

Gold rates change daily. The IBJA — India Bullion and Jewellers Association — publishes the official rate every morning. This is the rate your jewellery should be priced against.

Some showrooms in Gurgaon refuse to confirm gold rates over the phone — a practice that frustrates buyers and prevents informed decision-making before visiting. There is no legitimate reason for this. The rate is public information.

Before visiting any showroom, check the day's IBJA rate yourself at ibjarates.com. Then call the showroom and confirm they are using the IBJA rate. If they are not, ask why.
