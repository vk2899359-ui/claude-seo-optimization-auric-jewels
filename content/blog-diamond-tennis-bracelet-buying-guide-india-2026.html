What Makes a Tennis Bracelet Special

Unlike other bracelets that feature mixed elements, a tennis bracelet is defined by its uniformity — identical or near-identical diamonds, evenly spaced, in a single continuous line. This creates a unbroken ribbon of sparkle around the wrist. The effect is mesmerising: every movement of the hand creates a cascade of light that catches attention without demanding it.

Tennis Bracelet Styles in 2026

Classic Prong-Set

Each diamond is held by 3 or 4 prongs, allowing maximum light exposure. This is the most brilliant style, as the prongs create minimal obstruction. The classic choice for a reason.

Bezel-Set

Each diamond is surrounded by a thin metal rim. Lower profile than prong-set, less prone to snagging on clothing. Slightly more contemporary look with a sleek, modern feel. Ideal for daily wear.

Channel-Set

Diamonds are set between two parallel walls of metal. The smoothest surface — no prongs or bezels to catch. Most durable for everyday wear but slightly less brilliant due to metal coverage.

Graduated

Diamonds increase in size from the clasp toward the centre, with the largest diamond at the top of the wrist. Creates a dramatic focal point. Often more affordable because smaller stones are used on the sides.

How to Choose the Right Tennis Bracelet

Total Carat Weight

The total carat weight (TCW) is the combined weight of all diamonds in the bracelet. A 3.00 ct TCW bracelet with 40 diamonds has an average of 0.075 ct per stone — each one is small, but together they create substantial sparkle. A 5.00 ct TCW bracelet with the same number of diamonds has 0.125 ct per stone — noticeably larger and more impressive.

Diamond Matching

In a tennis bracelet, uniformity is everything. All diamonds should match in colour, clarity, and size. Even one diamond that is noticeably different ruins the continuous line. At Auric Jewels, every tennis bracelet is hand-assembled with matched stones selected by our gemologists.

Clasp Security

The clasp is the most important functional element. Look for a double-lock or box clasp with a safety catch. A tennis bracelet falling off during wear is a loss that is difficult to recover from. Every tennis bracelet at Auric Jewels features a double-safety clasp system.

Fit and Comfort

A tennis bracelet should sit snugly on the wrist with enough room to slide one finger underneath. Too tight is uncomfortable; too loose risks snagging and damage. Standard lengths are 7 inches (18 cm) for women and 8 inches (20 cm) for men. At Auric Jewels, we measure your wrist and customise the length to ensure perfect fit.
