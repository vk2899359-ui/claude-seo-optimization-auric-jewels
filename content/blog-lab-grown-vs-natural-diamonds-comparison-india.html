Lab-Grown vs Natural Diamonds — Complete Comparison Guide India 2026

Lab-grown diamonds and natural diamonds are chemically, physically, and optically identical — both are real diamonds made of pure crystallised carbon. The key difference lies in their origin: natural diamonds form over billions of years deep within the Earth's mantle, while lab-grown diamonds are created in controlled laboratory environments using advanced technology in weeks. What Are Lab-Grown Diamonds?

Lab-grown diamonds — also called synthetic diamonds, cultured diamonds, or man-made diamonds — are produced using two primary methods. High Pressure High Temperature (HPHT) replicates the extreme conditions under which natural diamonds form, while Chemical Vapour Deposition (CVD) grows diamonds layer by layer from a carbon-rich gas. Both methods produce diamonds that are indistinguishable from natural diamonds to the naked eye and even under standard gemological testing. Only specialised laboratory equipment can differentiate between the two.

It is important to note that lab-grown diamonds are not the same as diamond simulants like cubic zirconia or moissanite. Lab-grown diamonds are real diamonds with the same hardness (10 on the Mohs scale), refractive index, and thermal conductivity as natural diamonds.

Price Difference — Lab-Grown vs Natural Diamonds in India 2026

The price gap between lab-grown and natural diamonds has been one of the most discussed topics in India's jewellery market. Here is what buyers in Gurgaon and Delhi NCR need to know about pricing in 2026.

Current Price Comparison

As of 2026, lab-grown diamonds are approximately 70 to 85 per cent less expensive than equivalent natural diamonds. A 1-carat, G-colour, VS1 clarity natural diamond that costs ₹3,00,000 to ₹4,50,000 would have a lab-grown equivalent priced at approximately ₹45,000 to ₹90,000. This price gap has widened significantly over the past three years as production technology has matured and supply has increased.

Price Trends

Natural diamond prices have remained relatively stable or appreciated modestly over the long term, supported by controlled supply from major mining companies. Lab-grown diamond prices, by contrast, have declined steadily as production scales up. This trend is expected to continue, which has significant implications for resale value — a critical factor for Indian buyers who view diamond jewellery as both adornment and investment.

IGI Certification — What to Look For

The International Gemological Institute (IGI) is the most widely used certification body for diamonds in India. Both natural and lab-grown diamonds can be IGI certified, and the reports follow the same rigorous grading standards. However, there are important differences in how the reports are presented.

Natural Diamond Reports

An IGI natural diamond report includes a detailed assessment of the 4Cs, a clarity plot showing inclusions, and a statement confirming the stone is natural. The report number can be verified on the IGI website.

Lab-Grown Diamond Reports

An IGI lab-grown diamond report follows the same format but clearly states "Laboratory Grown Diamond" on the report. The stone is also laser-inscribed on its girdle with the report number and the words "Lab Grown." This ensures full transparency for the buyer and any future owner.

Which Should You Choose? A Decision Framework

The right choice between lab-grown and natural diamonds depends entirely on your purpose, priorities, and values. Here is a practical framework to guide your decision.

Choose Natural Diamonds If:

You want long-term value retention. Natural diamonds hold their value and can be exchanged, upgraded, or resold. For engagement rings, bridal jewellery, and heirloom pieces that will be passed down through generations, natural diamonds are the choice that preserves both emotional and financial value.

You are purchasing for a milestone occasion. Engagements, weddings, and significant anniversaries carry emotional weight that many families feel is best matched by a natural diamond — a stone that took billions of years to form.

You view jewellery as investment. Indian families have traditionally viewed diamond jewellery as a store of value. For this purpose, certified natural diamonds of good quality remain the only option with a proven secondary market.

Choose Lab-Grown Diamonds If:

You want a larger stone within a specific price range. Lab-grown diamonds allow you to purchase a significantly larger or higher-quality stone for the same investment. If visual impact is your priority and resale value is not a concern, lab-grown diamonds deliver exceptional value.

You are purchasing fashion or trend-driven jewellery. For pieces that follow current trends and may not be worn for decades, lab-grown diamonds offer the beauty of real diamonds without a significant financial commitment.

Environmental considerations are important to you. Lab-grown diamonds have a smaller environmental footprint than mined diamonds, though this advantage varies depending on the energy source used in production.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are lab-grown diamonds real diamonds?

Yes. Lab-grown diamonds are chemically, physically, and optically identical to natural diamonds. They are made of pure crystallised carbon and have the same hardness, brilliance, and fire as natural diamonds. The only difference is their origin — laboratory versus Earth's mantle.

Can a jeweller tell the difference between lab-grown and natural diamonds?

Not with standard gemological tools. Lab-grown and natural diamonds look identical under a jeweller's loupe and even under standard microscopy. Only specialised equipment (such as a DiamondView or spectroscopy device) can detect the subtle growth patterns that distinguish the two. This is why certification from IGI or GIA is essential — it clearly identifies the diamond's origin.

Do lab-grown diamonds hold their value in India?

Currently, lab-grown diamonds have very limited resale value in India. As production technology improves and supply increases, prices have declined steadily. Most established jewellers do not offer buyback or exchange on lab-grown diamonds. If long-term value retention is important to you, natural certified diamonds remain the recommended choice.
