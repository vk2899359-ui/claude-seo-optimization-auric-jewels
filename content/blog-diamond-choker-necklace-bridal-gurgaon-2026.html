The Rivière Choker

The rivière — a single continuous row of graduated diamonds set in a uniform line — is the choker in its most classical, refined expression. Each diamond is matched for colour, clarity, and cut, creating a seamless ribbon of light that sits with extraordinary elegance against the décolletage. Our rivière chokers at Auric Jewels are crafted in 18K white gold with IGI-certified round brilliant diamonds, carefully graduated so the largest stone anchors the centre with perfect visual harmony.

For the bride who values timelessness above all else, the rivière choker is the unambiguous choice. It complements a sheer silk saree as naturally as it does an embellished lehenga. It will look as impeccable at a silver anniversary dinner as it does on the wedding day itself.

Pairing Your Diamond Choker: From Ceremony to Celebration

One of the most important conversations a bride can have with her jeweller concerns how a single piece will anchor multiple looks across the wedding events. The diamond choker's versatility is its most practical luxury.

For the wedding ceremony, pair a diamond choker with longer diamond earrings — chandelier drops or classic jhumkas — to maintain visual balance. The choker's horizontal emphasis draws the eye across the collarbone, while length in the earrings creates vertical elegance that flatters every face shape.

For the reception, allow the choker to stand alone. A sleek diamond choker worn with diamond studs or simple drop earrings creates a sophisticated, high-fashion look that requires no further ornamentation. Let the certified diamonds speak for themselves.

For post-wedding events — sangeet, mehendi, or family lunches — a diamond choker worn with a day saree or fusion Indo-Western outfit brings a refined formality that distinguishes without overpowering. This is jewellery that never disappears into a occasion; it elevates every one.
