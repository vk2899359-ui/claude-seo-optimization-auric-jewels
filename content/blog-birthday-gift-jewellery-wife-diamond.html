How to Pick the Right Piece Without Her Knowing

Study What She Wears

Look at the jewellery she reaches for every morning. Does she wear studs or drops? Gold or silver tone? Delicate or bold? Her daily choices reveal her true preference better than any conversation.

Check Her Jewellery Box

What is missing? If she has rings and earrings but no bracelet — a tennis bracelet fills the gap. If she has gold pieces but no diamonds — diamond studs or a pendant add a new dimension to her collection.

Mistakes to Avoid

  • Don't buy fashion jewellery: Cubic zirconia and gold-plated pieces say "afterthought." Certified diamond and hallmarked gold say "I chose this carefully."
  • Don't guess her ring size: If you are unsure, choose a pendant, bracelet, or earrings instead. Or discreetly borrow one of her existing rings for sizing.
  • Don't over-complicate the design: Simple, elegant pieces are worn more often than elaborate, ornate designs. A solitaire pendant will see more wear than a statement necklace.
