What Determines the Price — The 4Cs Explained

Cut — The Most Important Factor

Cut determines how well the diamond returns light to your eye. An Excellent cut 1 carat diamond creates fire, brilliance, and scintillation that makes the stone look alive. A poorly cut 1 carat diamond looks dull and lifeless, even if the colour and clarity are high. At Auric Jewels, we only stock Excellent and Very Good cut diamonds — because cut is where value lives.

Price impact: An Excellent cut 1 carat diamond costs 10-15% more than a Very Good cut, but the visual difference is dramatic.

Clarity — What You Can Actually See

Clarity measures inclusions (internal imperfections) visible under 10x magnification. The scale runs from IF (Internally Flawless) to I3 (heavily included). The practical truth: VS2 and SI1 clarity diamonds appear clean to the naked eye. You cannot see the difference between a VS2 and a VVS1 without a loupe — but you will pay 30-40% less for the VS2.

Colour — How White Is White Enough?

Diamond colour is graded D (completely colourless) to Z (noticeable yellow tint). For 1 carat diamonds in white gold settings, G and H colour grades offer the best value — they appear white to the untrained eye and cost 20-30% less than D-E grades. In yellow gold or rose gold settings, you can comfortably go to I-J colour because the warm metal tone masks any slight tint.

Carat — The 0.90 ct Hack

Here is a secret that jewellers do not advertise: a 0.90 carat diamond is visually indistinguishable from a 1.00 carat diamond to the naked eye. The diameter difference is less than 0.3mm. But the price difference is 15-20%. If maximising value matters, ask your jeweller at Auric Jewels to show you 0.90-0.99 ct options alongside 1.00 ct stones. Most buyers cannot tell the difference.

A 1 carat diamond ring starts from approximately ₹1,50,000 for a good quality stone (H-I colour, SI1-SI2 clarity, Very Good cut) in 18K gold. Prices below this typically indicate uncertified stones or lower cut quality.

Is a 1 carat diamond ring a good investment?

A certified (IGI/GIA) 1 carat diamond holds value well and can be exchanged or upgraded over time. At Auric Jewels, we offer lifetime exchange on solitaires — you can upgrade to a larger or higher-quality stone at any time, paying only the difference.

How big does a 1 carat diamond look on the finger?

A round brilliant 1 carat diamond measures approximately 6.5mm in diameter. It is noticeably larger than a 0.50 ct (5.2mm) and makes a definite statement on the finger. Oval and pear shapes appear slightly larger than round for the same carat weight.

Should I buy IGI or GIA certified?

Both are internationally recognised and trusted. GIA is considered the gold standard globally, while IGI is widely respected and often more accessible for stones under 1 carat. At Auric Jewels, we stock both IGI and GIA certified diamonds.
