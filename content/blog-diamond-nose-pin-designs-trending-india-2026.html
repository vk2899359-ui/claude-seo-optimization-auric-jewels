Trending Nose Pin Designs 2026

1. Solitaire Diamond Stud

The classic — a single round brilliant diamond set in 18K gold. Clean, minimal, and endlessly versatile. This is the nose pin that goes with literally everything. At Auric Jewels, solitaire nose pin studs with IGI certified diamonds start from ₹8,000 (0.03 ct) to ₹50,000+ (0.15-0.25 ct).

2. Diamond Cluster Stud

Multiple small diamonds arranged in a floral or geometric pattern. Creates the appearance of a larger diamond at a fraction of the cost. A 7-stone flower cluster looks equivalent to a 0.15 ct solitaire but costs 40% less. Popular with brides and occasion wear.

3. Diamond Hoop (Nath Style)

A small hoop with diamond-set elements — inspired by the traditional nath but sized for daily wear. Hoops range from 8mm (subtle) to 14mm (noticeable). The diamond-set hoop is the fastest-growing nose pin category in 2026, bridging traditional and contemporary aesthetics.

4. Wire Nose Pin

A thin gold wire bent into a corkscrew or L-shape, with a diamond set at the visible end. The simplest design — the wire sits inside the nose and only the diamond shows. Extremely comfortable and secure. Available in all gold colours at Auric Jewels.

5. Marquise or Pear-Shaped Diamond

Fancy-shaped diamonds in marquise (pointed oval) or pear (teardrop) cuts create a distinctive look on the nose. These shapes appear larger than rounds for the same carat weight, offering more visual impact. Trending with fashion-forward buyers in Gurgaon.

6. Coloured Diamond Nose Pin

Champagne, yellow, and pink diamonds in nose pins are a rising trend in 2026. A warm champagne diamond nose pin in rose gold creates an unusual, eye-catching combination. Available by special order at Auric Jewels.

How to Choose the Right Nose Pin

Know Your Piercing Type

Different piercings require different nose pin backs:

  • Standard nostril piercing: Wire, screw-back, or L-shaped pin
  • Septum piercing: Small hoops or circular barbells
  • No piercing: Clip-on or press-on nose pins (available at Auric Jewels)

Choose the Right Metal

18K gold is recommended for nose pins because of its hypoallergenic properties. Avoid 22K gold for nose pins — it is too soft and can bend or distort inside the piercing. White gold creates a platinum-like appearance. Rose gold offers a warm, contemporary look.

Diamond Quality for Nose Pins

For nose pins, diamond cut is crucial — you want maximum sparkle from a small stone. At Auric Jewels, every nose pin diamond is Excellent or Very Good cut. Clarity can be SI1-SI2 (invisible at this size), and colour G-I works perfectly.

Is 18K or 22K gold better for nose pins?

18K gold is better for nose pins. It is harder (holds shape better in the narrow piercing), more hypoallergenic, and available in white and rose gold options. 22K gold is too soft for nose pin applications.
