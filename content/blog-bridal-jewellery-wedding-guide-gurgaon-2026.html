18K vs 22K Gold: Choosing the Right Purity for Your Bridal Set

The choice between 18K and 22K gold is one of the most consequential decisions in bridal jewellery planning. Both are available at Auric Jewels with full BIS hallmarking, and each has distinct advantages:

22K gold (916 hallmark) — 91.6% pure gold. The traditional and preferred metal for Indian bridal jewellery. Its deep yellow warmth complements the ceremonial aesthetic of wedding attire and is the metal in which most heritage Indian goldsmithing techniques — including Kundan, meenakari, and filigree — are executed. 22K gold is also the standard for heirloom value: its higher gold content makes it a superior long-term store of wealth.

18K gold (750 hallmark) — 75% pure gold, alloyed with metals that increase hardness. The preferred metal for diamond-set jewellery, as its higher durability protects prong settings and provides a neutral white or rose gold base that enhances diamond brilliance. Auric Jewels' solitaire diamond bridal sets are primarily crafted in 18K white gold or 18K yellow gold. The choice is aesthetic: white gold maximises diamond sparkle; yellow gold creates a warmer, more traditional look.

Many Gurgaon brides opt for a hybrid approach: 22K gold for their traditional ceremony jewellery (necklace, bangles, maang tikka) and 18K white gold for their diamond solitaire engagement ring and diamond earrings. Auric Jewels' bridal consultants will guide you through this decision based on your wedding outfits, skin tone, and personal aesthetic.

Solitaire Diamonds for Brides: A Guide to Choosing Right

The solitaire diamond has become the defining centrepiece of modern Indian bridal jewellery. An IGI-certified solitaire ring, presented as the engagement ring and worn through the wedding, carries both emotional and financial significance. Auric Jewels curates Gurgaon's most comprehensive solitaire diamond ring collection — every stone carrying full IGI or GIA certification covering the 4Cs: cut, colour, clarity, and carat weight.

When selecting a solitaire diamond for bridal wear, the following priorities apply:

  • Cut is the most important factor for brilliance. A well-cut diamond returns maximum light; an Excellent or Very Good cut grade is the standard at Auric Jewels.
  • Colour — for white diamonds, the D–F range (colourless) offers the greatest brilliance in white gold settings; G–H is an excellent choice for yellow gold, where the warm setting absorbs any subtle colour.
  • Clarity — VS1 or VS2 clarity offers eye-clean stones without the premium of Flawless grades. SI1 can be considered with expert guidance from an Auric Jewels gemologist.
  • Carat weight — meaningful size for bridal wear typically begins at 0.50ct; 1.00ct and above creates genuine presence. Auric Jewels carries solitaires from 0.30ct to 3.00ct and above.

Every solitaire purchase at Auric Jewels is accompanied by the original IGI or GIA certificate, a jewellery valuation report for insurance, and complimentary first cleaning within three months of purchase.

Understanding BIS Hallmarking and Diamond Certification

Certification is non-negotiable when investing in bridal jewellery. Auric Jewels' commitment to certification is the foundation of the trust families place in the brand:

BIS Hallmarking (Gold) — mandatory under Indian law since June 2021. Every gold piece at Auric Jewels carries the BIS logo, the fineness mark (916 for 22K, 750 for 18K), and a unique HUID number traceable on the BIS portal. Hallmarking guarantees that the gold purity you purchase is exactly what is described — no dilution, no ambiguity.

IGI Certification (Diamonds) — the International Gemological Institute is the world's leading diamond grading authority. Every diamond at Auric Jewels above 0.30ct carries an IGI certificate detailing cut grade, colour grade, clarity grade, and carat weight. This documentation is your permanent record of the stone's quality and its basis for insurance and resale valuation.

GIA Certification — for clients who prefer GIA (Gemological Institute of America) certified diamonds — widely considered the most conservative and internationally recognised grading standard — Auric Jewels maintains a curated GIA-certified inventory upon request.
