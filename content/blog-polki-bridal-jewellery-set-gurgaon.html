What Is Polki, and Why Does It Command Such Reverence?

Polki is the art of setting uncut, natural diamonds — diamonds exactly as they emerge from the earth, before faceting — into gold, typically foil-backed to amplify their warm, diffused glow. The technique traces its lineage to the royal courts of Rajputana and the Mughal ateliers of Jaipur, where master karigars spent months — sometimes years — on a single set.

Unlike faceted diamonds, which scatter light in sharp flashes, Polki diamonds possess a softer, inner radiance. They do not compete with daylight; they inhabit it. On skin, under candlelight, draped against a heavy Benarasi or a Kanjeevaram silk, a Polki set does not glitter — it glows. This distinction is everything to a bride who has refined taste.

Polki vs. Kundan vs. Diamond: Understanding the Distinction

Brides frequently ask us to clarify the relationship between Polki, Kundan, and faceted diamonds. The distinctions are meaningful:

  • Polki uses uncut natural diamonds set in gold, often with a reflective foil backing. Each stone is unique in shape and carries the character of its geological origin.
  • Kundan is a setting technique using highly refined 24-karat gold foil to hold stones — traditionally glass, but at Auric Jewels, exclusively precious and semi-precious gemstones. Kundan and Polki are frequently combined into Polki Kundan bridal sets, considered the apex of Indian bridal craftsmanship.
  • Faceted Diamonds (brilliant-cut, princess-cut, oval) offer brilliance and precision. They are the language of international luxury. Many of our brides commission sets that combine faceted diamond centrepieces with Polki surrounds — the best of both worlds.

None of these choices is superior in an absolute sense. Each speaks a different dialect of beauty. At Auric Jewels, we guide every bride toward the set that speaks in her voice.

Caring for Your Polki Bridal Jewellery Set

Polki jewellery, unlike faceted diamonds, requires specific care to preserve the foil backing and the setting integrity. A few essential principles:

  • Store each piece individually in soft muslin or velvet — never loose in a shared box.
  • Keep away from moisture, perfume, and oil. Apply fragrance before wearing; remove jewellery before swimming or showering.
  • Clean only with a dry, soft brush. Never use ultrasonic cleaners or liquid solutions.
  • Return to Auric Jewels annually for a professional inspection and any re-foiling if needed — a service we provide complimentary for all pieces purchased from us.

With proper care, a Polki bridal set does not merely last a lifetime — it becomes the heirloom that defines the next generation's idea of beauty.
