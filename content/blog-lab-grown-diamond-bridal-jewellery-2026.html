What Are Lab-Grown Diamonds?

Lab-grown diamonds are real diamonds — chemically, physically, and optically identical to mined diamonds. They are created in a laboratory using two methods: HPHT (High Pressure High Temperature) and CVD (Chemical Vapor Deposition). Both produce genuine diamond crystals with the same carbon structure, hardness (10 on Mohs scale), and optical properties as natural diamonds. Even trained gemologists cannot distinguish them without specialised equipment.

The Case for Lab-Grown Bridal Jewellery

Dramatically More Jewellery for the Budget

This is the primary argument. A ₹5 lakh bridal budget buys a modest natural diamond set (2-3 ct total weight) or a spectacular lab-grown set (8-12 ct total weight). For brides who prioritise visual impact and wearing experience over investment value, the lab-grown option is compelling. You get a grander look for the same budget.

Ethical Positioning

Lab-grown diamonds avoid the environmental and ethical concerns associated with diamond mining. For value-driven couples, this alignment with personal principles adds meaning to the purchase.

Identical Beauty

No one — not your wedding guests, not your photographer, not your mother-in-law — can tell whether your bridal necklace features lab-grown or natural diamonds just by looking at it. The sparkle is identical. The photographs are identical. The compliments are identical.

The Case Against Lab-Grown Bridal Jewellery

Near-Zero Resale Value

This is the critical counterargument. Lab-grown diamond prices have fallen 60-70% over the past three years as production has scaled. A lab-grown diamond bought for ₹50,000 today may be worth ₹5,000-₹10,000 in five years. For Indian families who view bridal jewellery as an investment — a financial safety net for the bride — this depreciation undermines the fundamental purpose of the purchase.

Cultural and Family Expectations

In many Indian families, the bridal jewellery is an heirloom that passes between generations. Natural diamonds carry the weight of rarity and tradition. Lab-grown diamonds, while beautiful, do not carry the same generational significance. Family expectations around bridal jewellery value should be considered openly.

Unlimited Supply = Declining Prices

Natural diamonds are finite — the earth will not produce more. Lab-grown diamonds can be manufactured in unlimited quantities. As production scales and technology improves, prices will continue to fall. This is the opposite of what you want from a purchase that is meant to store value.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you tell the difference between lab-grown and natural diamonds?

Not with the naked eye. Even trained gemologists need specialised equipment (spectroscopy) to distinguish them. The difference is origin and value, not appearance or physical properties.

Will lab-grown diamond prices go up in the future?

Unlikely. As CVD and HPHT technology improves and more manufacturers enter the market, lab-grown diamond prices are expected to continue declining. This contrasts with natural diamonds, which have a finite supply.
