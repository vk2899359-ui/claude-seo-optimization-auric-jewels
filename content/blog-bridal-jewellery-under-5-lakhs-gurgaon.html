Smart Strategies to Maximise ₹5 Lakhs

Choose 18K Gold Over 22K for Diamond Pieces

18K gold is stronger than 22K (better for holding diamonds securely) and costs 18% less per gram. For your necklace, earrings, and ring — pieces that feature diamonds — 18K gold is the smarter choice. Reserve 22K for plain gold bangles where gold purity matters most. This single decision saves ₹15,000-₹25,000 on your total purchase.

Prioritise One Statement Piece

Allocate the bulk of your budget to the necklace — it is the most photographed and most visible piece. A beautiful necklace with certified diamonds compensates for simpler earrings and bangles. The reverse does not work: expensive earrings cannot compensate for a mediocre necklace.

Buy Convertible Designs

A necklace with a detachable pendant gives you a grand bridal necklace for the wedding and a delicate daily wear pendant after. At Auric Jewels, many bridal necklaces in the ₹1,50,000-₹2,00,000 range feature detachable elements designed specifically for this purpose.

Consider Pre-Owned or Heirloom Gold

If your family has ancestral gold jewellery, exchange it at Auric Jewels for full prevailing gold value. This dramatically increases your effective budget. Exchanging 50 grams of old 22K gold adds approximately ₹7,10,000 to your budget at current rates.

Focus on Certification, Not Carat Size

A well-cut 0.30 ct IGI certified diamond with Excellent cut grade will appear larger and more brilliant than a poorly cut 0.50 ct stone. At Auric Jewels, our consultants help you select stones that maximise visual impact within your budget.
