2. The Architectural Diamond Choker

Few pieces make as immediate an impression as a diamond-encrusted choker, and 2026 has firmly confirmed its return as the centrepiece of sophisticated bridal dressing. The most requested designs at our Gurgaon atelier are layered geometric chokers in 18K white gold, sitting precisely at the collarbone — commanding when paired with a deep-back blouse, equally refined with a structured reception saree. Our hand-crafted diamond chokers are available in natural certified stones, with total carat weights ranging from 8 to 22 carats depending on scale and occasion.

3. The Heritage-Inspired Diamond Maang Tikka

The maang tikka has resurfaced as a defining element of the 2026 bridal look. The finest iterations marry traditional temple motifs — peacock, lotus, floral jaali — with modern diamond settings that catch light from every angle. At Auric Jewels, our artisans work in 22K gold with rose-cut diamonds set in kundan-inspired frames, producing pieces that are unmistakably rooted in tradition while carrying the quiet confidence of contemporary refinement. These are the pieces brides' daughters will photograph and covet.

4. Statement Diamond Earrings

The gravitational centre of bridal jewellery has shifted decisively from the neck to the ear. Chandbalis with double-diamond halos, elongated jhumkas in oxidised gold with graduated diamond drops, and modern geometric drops with baguette accents are among the most requested designs at our studio. Each Auric signature ear piece is engineered for comfort across five to seven hours of wear — a practical luxury that is never, ever visible in the craftsmanship.

5. The Convertible Diamond Necklace

The most thoughtful bridal investment available in 2026 is a necklace designed to be worn two ways: a grand, multi-layered statement for the wedding day that separates cleanly into a shorter diamond choker and a pendant strand for post-wedding occasions. This convertible architecture transforms a single, significant investment into a lifetime wardrobe. The engineering behind such a piece is precise; the experience of wearing it is effortless.
