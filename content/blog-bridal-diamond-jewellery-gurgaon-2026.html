Four Principles for Buying Bridal Diamond Jewellery in Gurgaon

When you invest in bridal diamond jewellery, the decision deserves the same deliberation as any significant commitment. Four principles should govern every purchase:

Certification: Every diamond above 0.30 carats must carry independent certification — GIA, IGI, or HRD. A certificate confirms the stone's cut grade, colour, clarity, and carat weight as assessed by a gemologist with no commercial interest in the sale. Accept no substitutes.

Metal Quality: 18K gold (75% pure gold) provides superior structural integrity for intricate diamond settings and resists daily wear with greater resilience. 22K gold (91.6% pure) is the traditional choice for heritage and culturally significant pieces. All gold at Auric Jewels carries BIS hallmark certification.

Craftsmanship: Examine settings under magnification. Prong tips must be uniform and firmly secure each diamond. Pavé settings must be perfectly flush, with no raised or unevenly spaced stones. The quality of setting directly determines how long your diamonds remain secure and how brilliantly they perform.

Design Longevity: Your bridal jewellery will be photographed, worn again, and eventually passed down. Choose designs whose distinction comes from proportion and quality rather than elaborate ornamentation. The finest bridal pieces remain beautiful in every decade, not merely in the year they were purchased.
