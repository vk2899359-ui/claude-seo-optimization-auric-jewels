Care Tips for Daily Wear Gold Jewellery

Gold is durable, but daily wear pieces need proper care to maintain their lustre and longevity. Follow these expert-recommended care practices.

Daily Habits

Remove your gold jewellery before applying perfume, moisturiser, or sunscreen. Chemical compounds in these products can dull gold surfaces over time. Put your jewellery on last when getting ready and take it off first when you return home.

Weekly Cleaning

Soak your daily wear gold pieces in warm water with a few drops of mild dish soap for 15 to 20 minutes. Gently brush with a soft-bristled toothbrush, rinse under clean water, and pat dry with a lint-free cloth. This removes accumulated oils and residue that dull the surface.

Storage

Store each piece separately in a soft pouch or lined jewellery box to prevent scratches. Avoid tossing multiple pieces into a single compartment — gold can scratch gold. For diamond-studded pieces, individual compartments are essential to prevent stones from chipping or loosening.

Professional Maintenance

This preventive care extends the life of your jewellery significantly.
