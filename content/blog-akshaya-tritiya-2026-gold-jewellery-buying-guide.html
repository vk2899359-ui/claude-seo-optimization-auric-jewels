Red Flags to Avoid When Buying Gold on Akshaya Tritiya

Unchecked Hallmarking

Since June 2021, every piece of gold jewellery sold in India must carry a BIS hallmark with a HUID number. Verify this on the BIS website before paying. Auric Jewels provides HUID verification for every piece at the billing counter.

Inflated Making Charges During Festive Rush

Some showrooms increase making charges during high-demand periods like Akshaya Tritiya. Always ask for a written price breakdown before committing. Compare the making charge percentage, not just the final price.

Old Stock Passed Off as New

Ensure the jewellery you buy has a current hallmark. Older stock may carry outdated hallmarks or none at all. At Auric Jewels, every piece in the showroom carries current-year BIS certification.
