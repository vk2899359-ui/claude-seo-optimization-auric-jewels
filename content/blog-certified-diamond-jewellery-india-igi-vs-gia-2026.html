How to Buy Certified Diamond Jewellery in India — IGI vs GIA Guide 2026

Certification is the single most important factor when buying diamond jewellery in India. A certified diamond comes with an independent, internationally recognised report that verifies the stone's quality — cut, clarity, colour, and carat weight. An uncertified diamond is a gamble. Yet many Indian buyers still purchase diamonds without proper certification, often paying more for lower quality. This guide from Auric Jewels explains the two major certifications — IGI and GIA — and how to use them to protect your purchase.

Why Certification Matters

Without an independent certification report, you are relying entirely on the jeweller's word for a diamond's quality. This creates three risks:

  • Overpaying: A jeweller may describe a J colour, SI2 clarity diamond as "near-colourless, eye-clean" — technically not wrong, but misleading. With certification, you know exactly what you are buying and can compare prices across showrooms.
  • No resale baseline: If you ever want to exchange, upgrade, or sell your diamond, a certification report establishes its market value. Without it, buyers have no reference point.
  • No accountability: If the diamond does not match what was promised, a certification report is your proof. Without it, disputes become he-said-she-said.

Which Should You Choose?

For most diamond jewellery purchases in India, both IGI and GIA certifications are fully trustworthy. GIA is marginally stricter — a diamond graded G colour by GIA might be graded F by IGI. This means GIA-certified diamonds sometimes appear to be better value because the grade is conservative.

How to Read a Diamond Certification Report

Key Fields to Check

  1. Report number: Every certification has a unique number. Verify it on the lab's website (igi.org or gia.edu) to confirm authenticity.
  2. Carat weight: Measured to two decimal places. A "1 carat" diamond should read 1.00 or higher.
  3. Cut grade: Look for Excellent or Very Good. Avoid Good, Fair, or Poor cuts.
  4. Colour grade: D is best, Z is worst. G-H offers the best value for most buyers.
  5. Clarity grade: FL is best, I3 is worst. VS2-SI1 is the sweet spot for value.
  6. Clarity plot: A diagram showing the exact location and type of inclusions.
  7. Fluorescence: None to Medium is fine. Strong or Very Strong fluorescence can affect appearance.
  8. Proportions: Table percentage, depth percentage, crown angle — these determine how well the diamond sparkles.

Red Flags — What Certification Is NOT

In-House Certificates

Some jewellers issue their own "certificate of quality" or "purity guarantee." This is not diamond certification. An in-house certificate is the jeweller grading their own product — there is no independent verification. Always insist on IGI or GIA certification from an independent laboratory.

SGL and Other Indian Labs

SGL (Solitaire Gemological Laboratories) is an Indian lab that certifies diamonds. While legitimate, SGL grading tends to be more generous than IGI or GIA. If you are comparing diamonds, compare within the same lab system. An SGL "G colour" may be an IGI "H colour" or GIA "I colour."

Laser-Inscribed Diamonds Without Reports

Some diamonds have a report number laser-inscribed on the girdle (edge). This is a good sign — but always cross-check the number on the lab's website. A laser inscription without a matching report in the lab's database is worthless.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is IGI certification accepted everywhere in India?

Yes. IGI is the most widely accepted diamond certification in India, recognised by every major jeweller and exchange. GIA is equally accepted. Both provide credibility for resale and exchange transactions.

Does certification guarantee the diamond is natural?

Yes. Both IGI and GIA reports clearly state whether the diamond is natural or laboratory-grown. This is one of the key protections certification provides — ensuring you know exactly what you are buying.

How much does certification cost?

At Auric Jewels, certification cost is included in the diamond price. You should never pay separately for a certification report. If a jeweller charges extra for certification, consider it a red flag.

Can I get an existing diamond certified?

Yes. If you have an uncertified diamond (from family jewellery, for example), you can submit it to IGI or GIA for independent grading. At Auric Jewels, we can facilitate this process for customers who want to certify heirloom diamonds.
