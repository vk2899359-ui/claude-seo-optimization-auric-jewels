The Four Pillars of a Perfect Solitaire

Cut — The Soul of the Diamond

No characteristic influences a solitaire's brilliance more than its cut. An Excellent or Ideal cut grade ensures that light enters the diamond and returns through the crown in a spectacular display of fire and scintillation. At Auric Jewels, every solitaire diamond ring in Gurgaon is hand-selected for cut quality — because a diamond that does not dance in light is simply not worthy of your story. We do not carry diamonds graded below Very Good, and our consultants will demonstrate the visible difference under natural and LED light before you decide.

Clarity — Rarity Made Visible

Clarity refers to the presence or absence of internal inclusions. For a solitaire, where the stone is the centrepiece and stands alone in its setting, we recommend VS1 to VS2 clarity grades — inclusions invisible to the naked eye, diamonds that appear flawless at every angle and in every photograph that will document this chapter of your life. SI1 stones with no eye-visible inclusions can also offer exceptional value for larger carat weights, and our gemologists will advise based on your specific stone selection.

Colour — The Language of Warmth

Colourless diamonds (D–F grade) project an icy, imperial brilliance that is unmatched in white gold or platinum settings. Near-colourless stones (G–H) offer exceptional value while appearing equally stunning to the untrained eye — and when set in 18K yellow gold or rose gold, exhibit a warmth that feels quintessentially luxurious. Our gemologists at Auric Jewels walk every client through colour grades under consistent natural light so you can make your decision with complete, unhurried confidence.

Carat — Presence That Commands Attention

In Gurgaon's luxury market, the most sought-after solitaire diamond rings in 2026 range from 0.70ct to 2.00ct — stones large enough to command presence on the hand without veering into ostentation. The right carat weight is deeply personal, shaped by the wearer's finger size, lifestyle, and aesthetic sensibility. Our consultants will guide you to the carat that feels inevitable, not just impressive.

The Art of the Setting — 2026's Most Desired Styles in Gurgaon

A solitaire diamond does not exist in isolation — it lives within its setting, and the right choice of mount transforms a stone into a statement.

The Classic Six-Claw Prong (Tiffany-Style)

The most iconic solitaire setting, allowing maximum light to enter the stone from all angles. Six delicate prongs cradle the diamond while exposing it generously, creating the illusion of a floating stone. Timeless, feminine, and universally flattering on every hand shape.

The Cathedral Setting

Elegant arches of metal rise to support the centre stone, adding height and a regal silhouette. Particularly striking in 18K yellow gold, the cathedral setting gives the ring a commanding presence that photographs exceptionally well.

The Bezel Setting

A sleek rim of metal encircles the diamond's girdle — contemporary, secure, and ideal for women with active lifestyles who desire a profile that will endure decades of daily wear without sacrificing sophistication.

The Hidden Halo

A micro-pavé halo of smaller diamonds sits beneath the centre stone, invisible from above but revealed in profile — a whispered extravagance that adds brilliance while preserving the clean minimalism of the solitaire aesthetic. Among our most requested designs in Gurgaon in 2026.
