What to Check Before Buying Gold Jewellery in Gurgaon

1. BIS Hallmark with HUID

Non-negotiable. Since June 2021, every piece of gold jewellery sold in India must carry a BIS hallmark with a HUID (Hallmark Unique Identification) number. Verify this number on the BIS website. If a showroom sells unhallmarked gold — leave immediately.

2. Itemised Billing

Your invoice should clearly separate: gold weight, gold rate per gram, making charges (amount and percentage), wastage (if any), diamond charges (if applicable), and GST. If the bill shows only a total number without breakdown — you have no way to verify fair pricing.

3. Making Charge Percentage

Ask for the making charge as a percentage. Compare this across showrooms. A 5% difference on a ₹5,00,000 purchase is ₹25,000. At Auric Jewels, making charges start at 8% for plain gold and 12% for diamond-set pieces — among the most competitive in Gurgaon.

4. Exchange and Return Policy

Understand the exchange policy before buying. Key questions: What percentage of gold value do you receive on exchange? Is there a time limit? Can you exchange gold for diamond jewellery? At Auric Jewels, the answers are: full gold value, no time limit, and yes.

5. Diamond Certification (for Diamond Jewellery)

Every diamond above 0.20 carats should have an individual IGI or GIA certification report. Batch certificates for small accent diamonds are acceptable. In-house certificates are not independent verification and should not be relied upon.
