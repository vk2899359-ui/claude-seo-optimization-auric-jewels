Why Platinum Is the Preferred Metal for Men's Jewellery

Men's jewellery demands different qualities from women's jewellery. It needs to withstand daily wear, resist scratches from active lifestyles, and maintain its appearance over years without requiring frequent maintenance. Platinum meets these demands exceptionally well.

Exceptional Durability

Platinum is one of the densest and strongest precious metals. Unlike gold, which wears away over time and loses material, platinum displaces rather than erodes — meaning that a platinum ring worn daily for decades retains virtually all of its original metal weight. This makes platinum the most practical choice for men's jewellery that will be worn every day, from wedding bands to casual chains.

Hypoallergenic Properties

Platinum jewellery is typically 95 per cent pure (950 platinum), with only 5 per cent alloy metals. This high purity means platinum rarely causes skin reactions — a significant advantage for men who experience irritation from nickel or other alloy metals found in lower-purity gold or white gold. For men with sensitive skin, platinum is the safest precious metal choice.

Naturally White Lustre

Unlike white gold, which requires rhodium plating to achieve its white appearance (and needs re-plating every few years), platinum is naturally white. Its cool, silvery-white lustre does not fade, yellow, or require re-plating — ever. Over time, platinum develops a subtle matte patina that many men find appealing, as it gives the piece character and a lived-in quality. This patina can be polished away to restore the original high shine at any time.

Subtle Luxury

For men who prefer understated elegance over conspicuous display, platinum offers the perfect balance. Its colour is restrained and sophisticated — it does not announce itself the way yellow gold does, yet its weight, craftsmanship, and lustre are unmistakably luxurious to anyone who notices. This quality of quiet distinction is what draws professional men, executives, and grooms in Gurgaon to platinum.

Trending Platinum Jewellery Designs for Men in 2026

Men's platinum jewellery in 2026 balances masculine aesthetics with refined craftsmanship. Here are the categories and designs that are most sought-after.

Platinum Chains for Men

Platinum chains are the cornerstone of men's fine jewellery. In 2026, the most popular chain styles include:

Curb chains: The classic men's chain — flat, interlocking links with a bold, masculine profile. Available in widths from 3mm (subtle) to 8mm (statement).
Figaro chains: Alternating short and long links that create a rhythmic, elegant pattern. A refined choice for men who want something distinctive.
Box chains: Square-cut links that create a clean, geometric appearance. Popular with professionals for their modern, minimalist aesthetic.
Franco chains: V-shaped links that sit flat against the chest — one of the most durable chain types and ideal for daily wear.

Platinum chains for men typically weigh between 15 and 40 grams and are worn in lengths of 20 to 24 inches. The weight and density of platinum give these chains a substantial feel that lighter metals cannot replicate.

Platinum Bracelets for Men

Platinum bracelets are the fastest-growing category in men's fine jewellery in 2026. The trending designs include:

Link bracelets: Chunky, interlocking links with a satin or brushed finish — the most popular style for everyday wear.
ID bracelets: A flat rectangular plate connected to a chain, often engraved with initials or a meaningful date. A classic that has been reimagined in platinum for 2026.
Cuff bracelets: Solid platinum bands that open at one side, worn snug against the wrist. The most architectural and statement-making option.
Cuban link bracelets: Tightly interlocking round links that create a smooth, rope-like chain — a luxurious everyday piece.

Men's platinum bracelets typically weigh between 20 and 50 grams and are worn at 7.5 to 8.5 inches in length, depending on wrist size.

Platinum Rings for Men

Platinum rings for men in 2026 go well beyond the classic wedding band. The trending designs include:

Classic bands: Polished or matte-finished bands in widths from 4mm to 8mm — the timeless choice for wedding rings and everyday wear.

Diamond-accented bands: Subtle channel-set or flush-set diamonds in a platinum band — adding understated brilliance for grooms and style-conscious men.
Signet rings: A platinum update on the classic gentleman's ring, often featuring an engraved monogram or family crest.
Two-tone rings: Platinum combined with rose gold or yellow gold inlays — a modern design that adds warmth and contrast.

Caring for Platinum Men's Jewellery

Platinum's durability makes it remarkably low-maintenance, but proper care extends its beauty and longevity.

Daily care: Remove platinum jewellery before heavy physical work, gym sessions, or contact sports to minimise scratching. While platinum does not lose metal when scratched, excessive scratching dulls the surface faster.
Cleaning: Soak in warm water with mild soap for 20 minutes, then brush gently with a soft toothbrush. Rinse and dry with a lint-free cloth. Do this weekly for chains and bracelets worn daily.
Professional polishing: Visit Auric Jewels once or twice a year for professional polishing that restores the original high shine. This complimentary service removes the accumulated patina and brings back the brilliant lustre.
Storage: Store platinum pieces separately from gold jewellery — platinum can scratch softer metals. Use individual pouches or compartments in a lined jewellery box.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is platinum jewellery suitable for daily wear by men?

Yes. Platinum is one of the most durable precious metals and is ideal for daily wear. Unlike gold, platinum does not lose metal when scratched — it displaces, meaning the metal moves rather than wears away. A platinum chain, bracelet, or ring can be worn every day for decades and still retain virtually all of its original weight and integrity.

How does platinum compare to silver in men's jewellery?

Platinum and silver look similar in colour but differ dramatically in quality. Silver tarnishes, scratches easily, and is not suitable for long-term daily wear. Platinum is 30 times rarer than gold, does not tarnish, and is significantly more durable. For fine jewellery that will be worn regularly, platinum is in an entirely different category from silver.

What weight of platinum chain is recommended for men?

For daily wear, a platinum chain weighing 15 to 25 grams offers a comfortable balance of substance and wearability. For a more prominent look, chains of 25 to 40 grams provide a bolder presence. The ideal weight depends on your build, personal style, and whether the chain will be worn under or over clothing.

Is platinum hypoallergenic?

Yes. Platinum jewellery is typically 95 per cent pure, with only 5 per cent alloy metals (usually iridium or ruthenium, which are also hypoallergenic). This makes platinum the safest precious metal for men with sensitive skin or nickel allergies — a common concern with white gold, which often contains nickel in its alloy.

Does platinum jewellery require special maintenance?

Platinum is remarkably low-maintenance. It does not tarnish, fade, or require re-plating like white gold. Over time, it develops a subtle matte patina that many wearers appreciate.
