Auric Jewels -- Search Engine Ping & Indexing Tool

Notify search engines about your blog pages and request faster indexing

How to Use This Tool

  1. Ping Sitemap: Click the "Ping Google Sitemap" button below to notify Google that your sitemap has been updated. Do this every time you publish a new blog post.
  2. Request Indexing: For each blog URL, click "Inspect in GSC" to open it in Google Search Console's URL Inspection tool. Then click the "Request Indexing" button on that page.
  3. Open Blog Page: Click "Visit Page" to open the blog post in a new tab and verify it is loading correctly.
  4. Webmaster Tools: Use the links at the bottom to access Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools directly.
