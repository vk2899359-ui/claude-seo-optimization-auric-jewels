## Step 4: Top 5 Keywords to Target First (Within 3 Months)

**Selection criteria:** High local Gurgaon intent · Lower competition · Achievable ranking · Conversion potential

### ✅ Priority 1 — `jewellery shop sector 45 gurgaon`
- **Why:** Auric is *already* appearing (blog pages). Converting blog visibility into a proper landing page / GMB optimisation can push this to position 1.
- **Volume:** 100–300/mo | **Difficulty:** Low | **Intent:** Visit/Buy
- **Action:** Create a dedicated `/sector-45-gurgaon` store page with NAP, opening hours, photos, schema markup.

### ✅ Priority 2 — `best jewellery showroom gurgaon`
- **Why:** Medium difficulty, dominated by listicle/directory sites — not major e-commerce brands. A well-optimised "About / Showroom" page with structured data can break into top 5 within 60–90 days.
- **Volume:** 500–1,000/mo | **Difficulty:** Medium | **Intent:** Compare → Buy
- **Action:** Publish a long-form page targeting this keyword with trust signals (certifications, reviews, photos).

### ✅ Priority 3 — `diamond earrings gurgaon`
- **Why:** Lower competition than rings; BlueStone and Ophira dominate but both lack dedicated local landing pages. Auric can outrank with a product category page + local schema.
- **Volume:** 500–800/mo | **Difficulty:** Medium | **Intent:** Buy
- **Action:** Create `/diamond-earrings-gurgaon` category page with product listings and localised content.

### ✅ Priority 4 — `diamond necklace gurgaon`
- **Why:** SERP is dominated by aggregator sites (WedMeGood, Sulekha), not brand websites — a direct product page from Auric can outrank these with proper on-page SEO.
- **Volume:** 400–700/mo | **Difficulty:** Medium | **Intent:** Buy
- **Action:** Create `/diamond-necklace-gurgaon` with product grid, pricing signals, and IGI/GIA certification mentions.

### ✅ Priority 5 — `bridal jewellery gurgaon`
- **Why:** High seasonal spikes (wedding season Oct–Feb). CaratLane and Tanishq dominate nationally but local-intent bridal searches reward local showrooms with trust content.
- **Volume:** 1,000–2,000/mo | **Difficulty:** Hard (→ Medium for local) | **Intent:** Buy/Compare
- **Action:** Publish a dedicated Bridal Collection page + blog series around Gurgaon wedding planning with Auric jewellery. Target long-tails like "bridal jewellery set gurgaon", "wedding necklace gurgaon".
